Biological age is measured at sea level. Most people don't live there.

A PhenoAge clinical clock re-fit for a mixed-ancestry cohort in Bogotá (2,600 m). Chronic hypoxia inflates the erythroid markers — RDW, MCV — and a sea-level clock reads it as accelerated aging. This corrects the measurement, not the person.
